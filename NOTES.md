### LEAD 1. FAA grounds every U.S. flight (AVIATION • USA) — Reuters
- FAA issued a nationwide ground stop ~3:54pm ET Fri 13 Nov, "due to
  extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety": all departing
  traffic to remain grounded until further notice.
- Cause: a telecommunication/communications outage hitting all Air
  Route Traffic Control Centers; technicians investigating. Officials:
  no indication of a cyberattack; not "another 9/11".
- Lifted ~8:15pm ET after ~4 hours. 1,000+ flights cancelled, ~10,000
  delayed (FlightAware via Reuters). 2nd nationwide ground stop of all
  departures since 9/11 (the other: Jan 2023, ~2h).
- Sources: northjersey.com, people.com, usatoday.com, palmbeachpost.com
  (all 13 Nov), Reuters figures via northjersey.
